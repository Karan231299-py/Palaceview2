A living
palace above
old Bundi

Nestled in the ancient lanes of Bundi's old city, Palace View sits directly beneath the towering ramparts of Garh Palace — one of Rajputana's most remarkable and least-known treasures.

From the rooftop, the palace fills the horizon in every direction of light: soft sandstone in the morning, warm amber at dusk, and a breathtaking illuminated spectacle after nightfall. Every hour is a different painting.

We offer an intimate rooftop restaurant, heritage rooms, and a view that reminds you why you travelled this far.

What Awaits You

Three ways to experience the palace

01 — Dining
Royal Rooftop
Restaurant
Open-air dining with an unobstructed view of Garh Palace. Rajasthani cuisine, craft teas, and a front-row seat to every sunset — served just the way the maharajas intended.
02 — The View
Palace by
Night
After sundown, Garh Palace transforms — warm gold floodlights illuminate 500 years of carved stone, domes, and arched galleries. There is no comparable view in Bundi.
03 — Stay
Heritage
Rooms
Wake to the palace bathed in morning light. Our heritage rooms combine old Bundi architecture with thoughtful comfort — each window a frame for something extraordinary.
